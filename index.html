Founder's note

Change starts in our own para.

ParaBondhu begins with a simple belief: people do not need to wait for a perfect system before they help someone nearby.

“

We always dreamt of bringing real change, not through big promises, but through small actions that actually help people.

When the world is busy fighting over politics, power, and geopolitical conflicts, maybe change should start closer to home, with our own people, in our own para.

ParaBondhu is born from that belief: to listen to local problems, support those who are often ignored, and stand beside people when they need help the most.

We may start small, but one solved problem, one helped student, one supported family can still become the beginning of real change.

Be the change. Join us.

Bartik Saha,Founder.

About

Why ParaBondhu Exists

Many local problems remain ignored because people do not know where to report them, whom to trust, or how to bring attention to them. ParaBondhu is built to create a simple bridge between local issues and people who care. From broken roads to struggling students, from unsafe corners to families needing support, we believe every report matters when it is genuine.

Local attention

Reports help bring visibility to the streets, lanes, families, and students that often go unseen.

Verified support

ParaBondhu keeps the human story close while checking details before volunteers or networks act.

Volunteer / Support

Become a Local Hero

You do not need power, money, or a big position to help. Sometimes, sharing a report, verifying a location, guiding a student, donating books, or connecting someone with the right support can change a life.
